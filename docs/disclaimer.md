# Disclaimer

The findings, recommendations, and interpretations in this guide reflect the Project's own field research and the professional judgement of its implementation team. They do not necessarily represent the official position of GIZ or the German Federal Government.

This guide generalizes and anonymizes examples to protect participant, intermediary, and solution-provider confidentiality; reference codes replace names throughout. Depth of evidence varies by solution: [Section 5.7](05-insights.md#57-limitations-and-ethical-considerations) sets out exactly which findings carry full field validation and which are documented at roster level, and should be read before treating any single case study as representative.
