# Acknowledgements

This guide would not exist without the fieldwork, feedback, and trust of a great many people and organizations across Ghana.

Our thanks go to the fourteen intermediary organizations who carried out recruitment, training, co-creation facilitation, and field validation across six regions of Ghana over three years. They are identified by reference code throughout this guide to protect confidentiality, but their contribution is the evidentiary backbone of everything in these pages. See [Section 1.3](01-introduction.md#13-about-the-project-and-the-intermediary-cohort) for the coding scheme.

We thank the thirty solution-provider teams, across both Phase 1 (adapting existing products) and Phase 2 (building new ones), who opened their products, their assumptions, and often their business models to rigorous, sometimes uncomfortable, user testing.

Above all, we thank the hundreds of women micro-entrepreneurs across Ashanti, Eastern, Northern, North East, Upper East, and Upper West regions who gave their time, their honesty, and their trust to this project, usually without any direct benefit to themselves beyond the hope that the next version of these tools would work better for people like them.

This work was commissioned by the Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) GmbH and implemented by GFA Consulting Group GmbH.
